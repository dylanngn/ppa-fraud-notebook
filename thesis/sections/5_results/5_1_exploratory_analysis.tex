\section{Exploratory Analysis Results}
\label{sec:5_1}

This section details the patterns and insights discovered during the exploratory phase of our research, as outlined in the methodology (Section~\ref{sec:3_3}).

\paragraph{Temporal Pattern Discoveries}

Our analysis of the monthly fraud rate revealed a significant spike in February 2025, where the rate jumped to 13.12\%—a 62\% increase over the annual average of 8.11\%. Following this peak, the rate steadily declined to approximately 5\% by the middle of 2025. This downward trend likely reflects either successful interventions by the platform's security teams or a shift in fraudster activity toward other, less protected marketplaces.

When looking at hourly data, we confirmed a distinct activity peak between 6:00 and 7:00 AM. During this window, fraud rates were 40\% higher than the midday averages. Furthermore, weekend submissions carried a noticeably higher risk (9.2\% on Saturday and 8.8\% on Sunday) compared to the weekday average of 7.4\%, which aligns with periods of reduced manual oversight.

\paragraph{Geographic Pattern Insights}

Geographic origin remains one of the strongest indicators of fraudulent intent. Listings originating from specific West African countries—namely Benin, Togo, Ivory Coast, and Nigeria—accounted for 12\% of all fraudulent submissions, despite making up less than 0.5\% of the total volume. The fraud rates within these regions ranged from 60\% to 90\%, providing a high-precision signal for initial screening.

\paragraph{Entity-Sharing and Network Patterns}

The most compelling evidence of organized activity came from the analysis of shared device fingerprints. "Super-connector" devices—those linked to ten or more listings—showed a 35.5\% fraud rate, representing a 4.4-fold increase over the baseline. The most extreme case involved a single device fingerprint associated with 6,251 different listings, an unmistakable signature of a large-scale fraud ring. These observations were the primary motivation for the heterogeneous graph approach detailed in Section~\ref{sec:3_5}.
